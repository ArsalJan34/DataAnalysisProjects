- Performance dashboards

- Strategic business insights

Automatidata focuses on identifying business needs and using data to address them.

---

# 3. New York City TLC Project

Automatidata is consulting for the **New York City Taxi and Limousine Commission (TLC)**.

### TLC Role

The NYC TLC is responsible for:

- Licensing taxi cabs
- Regulating taxi cabs
- Regulating for-hire vehicles

### Project Goal

Automatidata is working toward developing a **regression model** that estimates taxi fares **before a ride** using TLC data.

### Dataset Scale

The TLC data comes from:

- 200,000+ taxi and limousine licensees
- Approximately 1 million combined trips per day

**Important:** The project dataset was created for educational purposes and may not represent actual NYC taxi rider behavior.
